Centauri Carbon dashboard · every state

Locked direction: 2a. Every screen below is the same live component, set to a different snapshot. You can use the controls on each one: tap Pause, Resume or Light, drag the stop thumb all the way to the right, or switch the theme. Simulated commands are acknowledged after about 1 s. To try failure and timeout outcomes, open the prototype. Components, tokens and design decisions are in the spec.

The clock in the mockups is fixed at 21:20. Times on screen are derived from updatedAt and remaining.
